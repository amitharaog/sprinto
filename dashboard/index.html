      How a company is qualified

      • Hard gates end the assessment: sells compliance automation, already uses a compliance platform (for example a trust centre hosted by Vanta), not software, consumer-only, holds both SOC 2 and ISO 27001, or is public or acquired.
      • Points: Series A–C +3, 20–500 people +2, enterprise signals +2, hiring for security or compliance +3, certification in progress +3, regulated industry +2, sells in the EU/UK +1, no certification +2, holds one of SOC 2 / ISO 27001 +1.
      • The agent loop reads extra pages (security, careers, pricing, customers) only for questions the homepage left open, at most 4 per company.
      • Contacts are ranked by role for the company's size band, then found with Hunter.io. They are shown here by role only; names are in the emails, which go to the Growth Lead's own inbox before any real send.
