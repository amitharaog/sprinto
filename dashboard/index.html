            The 7-step pipeline

            1. ScrapeReads the homepage politely: robots.txt checked, bot blocks recorded, not evaded—
            2. ExtractTurns page text into named fields with a quote for each—
            3. Qualify (agent loop)Fetches security, careers, pricing pages only for open questions—
            4. Find contactRanks roles by company size; Hunter.io finds the address—
            5. Pain pointsTwo per person, each tied to a scraped fact—
            6. Draft emailCode checks name, length, one ask and a real hook—
            7. SendTo the Growth Lead's own inbox, with review notes—

            Numbers are companies that completed each step in this run.

            How a company is qualified

            • Hard gates end the assessment: sells compliance automation, already uses a compliance platform (for example a trust centre hosted by Vanta), not software, consumer-only, holds both SOC 2 and ISO 27001, or is public or acquired.
            • Points: Series A–C +3, 20–500 people +2, enterprise signals +2, hiring for security or compliance +3, certification in progress +3, regulated industry +2, sells in the EU/UK +1, no certification +2, holds one of SOC 2 / ISO 27001 +1. Qualify at —.
            • The agent loop reads extra pages only for questions the homepage left open, at most 4 per company.
            • Contacts are ranked by role for the company's size band, then found with Hunter.io. If the top person has no findable address, the best-ranked reachable person is chosen. Shown here by role only; names are in the emails.
