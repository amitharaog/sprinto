Build 2 · Sprinto cold outreach

Outreach component map

Who does what, at which stage: from a list of domains to a reviewed email in the Growth Lead's inbox, and every decision on the dashboard. Code decides; Claude reads and writes; nothing reaches a prospect without a human.

Components

StepCodeWhat it does
1 · Scrape & cleanoutreach/step1_scrape.pyReads one page at a time. Checks robots.txt, records bot blocks instead of evading them, strips nav, footer and cookie banners, caps text at ~2k tokens, scans raw HTML for certification badges, caches pages for 7 days.
2 · Extract profileoutreach/step2_extract.pyClaude fills named fields (is it B2B SaaS, certifications held, stage, enterprise signals…) and quotes the page for each. Unknown stays empty.
3 · Qualify loopoutreach/step3_qualify.pyCode checks which ICP questions are still unanswered and fetches the next page that answers them (security, then careers, then pricing; at most 4). Hard gates end it early; a points table decides; Claude writes the why-now.
3b · Deal valueoutreach/step3b_value.pyEstimated contract range, win chance and expected value from company size (Hunter.io) and an editable price table.
3c · Future leadoutreach/step3c_future.pyFor every company that didn't qualify: near miss, watch for renewal, re-check now or not a fit, with a re-check date.
4 · Find contactoutreach/step4_poc.pyHunter.io domain search and email finder (cached). Roles ranked by company size; sales, HR and marketing never picked; must have a deliverable address.
5 · Pain pointsoutreach/step5_pain.pyClaude names two pains for that person's role, each tied to a scraped fact.
6 · Draft + checksoutreach/step6_email.pyClaude drafts; code checks first name, 60–140 words, one question, no placeholders or clichés, and that the hook is a real scraped fact. Up to 3 rewrites.
7 · Sendoutreach/step7_send.pyGmail to the Growth Lead's own inbox only, with review notes under the email. A .eml copy is always saved.
Dashboardoutreach/export_dashboard.py · dashboard/Decision traces exported to data.json and deployed on Vercel: Dashboard, Qualification, Pipeline value, Future leads, Decision trace.
